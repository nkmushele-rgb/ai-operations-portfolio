Background

Operations first, automation second

I spent over ten years in FMCG sales and account development, including route management and account development at Coastal Bottlers (Coca-Cola) and a sales officer role at Dutch Water Ltd. That work was daily reconciliation: orders against stock, deliveries against promises, and customers who noticed every gap.

In Qatar I worked in material security, stores and site operations with TRAGS, including oil and gas site exposure and permit receiver duties. Stores work teaches the same lesson as distribution: if the paperwork and the physical stock disagree, the paperwork is wrong.

I am now building practical AI automation on top of that experience. Not chatbots for their own sake — the boring, valuable parts: triaging enquiries so urgent ones are seen, keeping stock records that survive an audit, and making sure a serious lead does not sit unanswered behind a price request.

Everything on this page is something I built, ran and tested myself. The three projects below are portfolio work with synthetic data, not client deliveries, and each one states its own limitations.

What I am not claiming

  • These are portfolio projects with synthetic data, not paid client work.
  • No business results, savings or conversion figures are claimed. The demo numbers describe the demo.
  • The Make specs are validated structurally and their reference rules are executed with Node and cross-checked against the Python code. No scenario has been built or run inside Make.
  • The local model writes reply text. It does not make routing, scoring or escalation decisions, and the projects run without it.
  • I am not a senior AI engineer. I build practical automations, document them, and say where they stop.
