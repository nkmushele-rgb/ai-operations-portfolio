NEXORA Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

NEXORA is a personal, local-only tool used by its owner to manage authorized social media accounts through official platform APIs.

Data We Access

When you authorize a platform connection, NEXORA may access only the permissions you grant (for example, basic profile information and read-only account data) to provide the requested functionality.

How Data Is Used

Data is used only for account connection, read-only audits, and local app functionality. NEXORA does not sell personal data.

Storage

OAuth tokens and app data are stored locally on the owner's device. Secrets should be kept in local environment files and not shared publicly.

Sharing

Data is not shared with third parties except as required to communicate with the authorized platform APIs or when required by law.

Your Choices

You can revoke app access through the platform's security/settings page and disconnect the local connection in NEXORA.
