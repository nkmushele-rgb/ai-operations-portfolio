# Application letter templates

Replace every `[bracket]`. Delete any sentence that is not true for the role.
Never send a template unchanged.

## Before sending

- [ ] Read the advert and score it with `job-suitability.md`.
- [ ] Choose **one** project that answers its main requirement, not all three.
- [ ] Confirm the employment dates in the CV.
- [ ] Check the Make wording against `../DOCUMENTATION/make-verification.md`.
- [ ] Use the published portfolio URL: <https://nkmushele-rgb.github.io/ai-operations-portfolio/>.
- [ ] Keep rate and availability with the learner.

## 1. AI operations assistant / workflow automation

```
Subject: AI operations assistant — workflow automation portfolio

Hello [name],

I am applying for [role]. You asked for [requirement in their words]. I have
built a working example of that process rather than describing it in the
abstract.

In [project name], inbound [messages/leads/movements] are [validated],
classified by published rules, and held behind a human approval step before
anything is sent. The rules are written down, the result is logged, and 22–34
automated tests cover the failure cases. A local model writes the wording; it
does not make the decision. The project still works when the model is off.

My background is ten-plus years in FMCG sales, distribution and site operations,
so I know what happens when the record and the physical stock disagree. I am
moving that experience into automation deliberately, and I am not claiming past
client results.

I would be glad to start with a small paid trial on one of your processes. You
can see the projects, tests and limitations at [portfolio URL].

Kind regards,
Kelvin Mushele
```

## 2. Remote operations assistant with an inventory focus

```
Subject: Remote operations assistant — stock control experience

Hello [name],

Your role mentions [stock/inventory/record-keeping requirement]. I have two
kinds of relevant experience.

First, I worked in stores and material security on oil and gas sites with TRAGS,
including permit-to-work coordination, where the record has to match the
physical item. Before that I worked in FMCG distribution with Coastal Bottlers
and Dutch Water Ltd.

Second, I built a stock control project that records every receipt, issue and
adjustment, values stock at moving average cost, refuses an issue larger than
the balance, raises low-stock alerts and flags unusual movements. The sample run
posted 22 movements, refused one deliberate oversell and logged the reason. It
has 26 passing tests and a dashboard generated from that run.

I have not worked in [ERP/WMS name] — which system does your team use? I would
rather ask than guess. I can walk through the project at [portfolio URL] and
would start with a paid trial.

Kind regards,
Kelvin Mushele
```

## 3. Sales operations / lead management

```
Subject: Sales operations — lead triage and follow-up automation

Hello [name],

Your role mentions [lead handling / CRM / follow-up requirement]. I built a lead
management project that scores enquiries against six named rules, stores the
points breakdown on every lead, removes duplicates on both reference and email,
and writes a follow-up matched to the lead's band. Nothing sends until a named
person approves it, and a lost lead cannot be emailed at all.

That design came from sales work, not from a textbook: route management and
account development at Coastal Bottlers, and a sales officer role at Dutch Water
Ltd. A serious enquiry should not sit behind a price request.

I have not connected a live HubSpot instance yet; I would treat that as part of
a paid trial rather than claim it now. The project, its scoring rules and its 34
tests are at [portfolio URL].

Kind regards,
Kelvin Mushele
```

```
Hi [name] — I saw [role]. I build rule-based automations for [support/stock/leads]:
validated input, explicit decisions, a human approval step and tests for the
failure cases. Three working projects, synthetic data, no client claims.
Portfolio: [URL]. Would a small paid trial on one process be useful?
```

## Do not send

- "I saved a client 20 hours a week." — no client result exists.
- "I am a certified Make expert." — no certification and no live Make instance verified.
- "I have 5 years of HubSpot/SAP experience." — neither is evidenced.
- "I can start immediately in your time zone." — confirm availability first.
- Any attachment containing a real email address, phone number, lead list or token.
